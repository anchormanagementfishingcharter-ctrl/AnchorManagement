What a reservation does

  1. The form calls submitReservation() (lib/submitReservation.ts) -> POST /api/checkout.
  2. The server re-prices everything from the database (the browser's prices are ignored), checks stock for every date, and holds the gear for 35 minutes (reserve_booking in 00-schema.sql). Two people cannot take the last set.
  3. It saves the customer's signed agreement as a PDF in private storage and opens Stripe's payment page.
  4. Customer pays -> Stripe calls /api/stripe-webhook -> booking confirmed -> owner gets email + text, customer gets a receipt. If they abandon payment, the gear is freed automatically.
  5. Deposit: the card is saved at checkout. On delivery day a daily job (/api/deposit-holds) places a HOLD (not a charge). In /admin/bookings you tap "Gear returned: release deposit" or "Charge for damage".
  6. A daily job (/api/retry-alerts) resends any alert or receipt that failed.

Setup order

  1. Supabase SQL editor: run 00-schema.sql, then 01-database.sql, then 02-availability.sql, then 03-tags-media.sql (tags, videos, photos and their storage).
  2. Copy lib/, app/ and vercel.json into the Next.js project.
  3. npm i stripe zod pdf-lib resend twilio @supabase/supabase-js @supabase/ssr
  4. Environment variables (Vercel): STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, SUPABASE_URL, SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY, NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL, NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY, RESEND_API_KEY, ALERT_FROM_EMAIL (an address on your own domain, verified in Resend), TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID, TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN, TWILIO_FROM, ADMIN_EMAILS (who may open /admin), CRON_SECRET (long random string), SITE_URL (your live web address).
  5. Stripe webhook: https://YOUR-SITE/api/stripe-webhook, events checkout.session.completed AND checkout.session.expired.
  6. Register your Twilio number for US business texting (A2P 10DLC or toll-free verification). Can take days to weeks.
  7. Replace the sample text in lib/terms.ts with attorney-approved terms. Replace the starting products, prices, inventory, delivery cities and bait in 00-schema.sql with your real ones (or edit them in the database).

Admin area (/admin, owner only: ADMIN_EMAILS)

  • Bookings (new count badge, deposit release/charge), Prices, Package contents, Inventory (CSV import + tag numbers), Delivery cities and fees, Bait, Alerts (change the phone and email).
  • The reservation page reads /api/catalog (packages, prices, "includes" line, cities, bait) and /api/availability?start=&end= (how many of each package are left for the dates picked). Editing in the admin changes what customers see within a minute.

Tags, videos and photos

  • /admin/tags: "Assign tags from inventory" gives a delivered booking its tag numbers (RR-001...), with "All gear returned" or per-tag returns, an "Add a tag" box (for the cart or a swap), and a search by client or tag. A tag can only be out once.
  • /admin/videos: upload cooking and how-to videos (MP4, up to 50 MB; sent straight to storage). They show on /recipes and /howto.
  • /photos: anyone can upload a photo (first name + permission box). Limits: 5 per hour per visitor, JPG/PNG/WebP, 3 MB. /admin/photos: Approve or Reject. The newest 6 approved photos rotate as Catch of the Day.
  • Put <CatchOfTheDay /> (components/CatchOfTheDay.tsx) near the top of the home page.
  • Photos and videos are stored in public buckets under random file names, so a pending photo's link is not guessable but is not strictly private either. Do not accept anything sensitive.

Reservation page and photo alert

  • app/page.tsx (also served at /reserve) is the customer page: logo (put yours at public/logo.png), links, Catch of the Day, app/reserve/Reserve.tsx (packages, calendar, add-ons, delivery, bait, totals, agreement + signature, then Stripe) and the FWC license box.
  • New photo alert: when a customer photo reaches the approval queue you get an email every time, and a text only when the queue goes from empty to one photo. The Photos tab in /admin shows a count badge.

Rules built in (change in the settings table, key rental)

  • Rentals up to 7 days, because card holds usually last about 7 days. Deposit starts at $100.

  • Book, pay: you get the email + text, the customer gets the receipt, /admin/bookings shows it as New.
